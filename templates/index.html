The Problem

Too many talented students from underrepresented backgrounds give up on STEM careers because they don't see people like themselves succeeding in these fields, lack guidance on where to start, or face barriers that feel impossible to overcome. Without representation and clear pathways, potential is lost.

Our Solution

SeeMe in STEM uses AI to match students with personalized STEM career paths based on their unique interests and skills. We connect underrepresented students with relatable role models who share their backgrounds, bust common myths, and provide clear guidance on real STEM pathways — from the first step to career success.

The Impact

By helping underrepresented students see themselves in STEM careers early and providing representation with actionable guidance, we can close the diversity gap, unlock untapped potential, and ensure the next generation of innovators reflects the world we live in.

🚫 STEM Myths vs. ✨ Reality

Let's bust some common myths that might be holding you back!

MYTH

STEM is only for people who are naturally good at math.

TRUTH

Math is a skill you build with practice! STEM also needs creativity, teamwork, communication, and curiosity. Many successful STEM professionals weren't "math whizzes" at first.

MYTH

I need to know coding before college.

TRUTH

Most students learn coding for the first time in high school or college! Everyone starts somewhere, and there are tons of free resources to help you learn at your own pace.

MYTH

I don't see people like me in STEM.

TRUTH

STEM needs more diverse voices like yours! Role models from many backgrounds are already changing the field, and the industry is actively working to become more inclusive. You belong here!

MYTH

STEM careers are boring and isolating.

TRUTH

STEM is incredibly collaborative! You'll work on teams, solve real-world problems, create amazing things, and make a positive impact. Plus, STEM jobs offer flexibility and creativity!
